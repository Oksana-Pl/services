KD Beauty Space

A beauty studio website design with a homepage and a dedicated price list.

Introduce the treatments visually

Three service cards introduce permanent makeup, lashes and fine line tattoos. Photography sets the tone, while short descriptions and price links give each treatment a clear next step.

Make the price list easy to browse

A dedicated price page groups treatments by category. Category links at the top lead to the relevant table, with service names and prices aligned in separate columns.

Adapt the composition for a small screen

On mobile, the eye photograph sits above the studio introduction and booking button. The navigation collapses into a compact menu, while the serif display type and warm palette carry through from the desktop design.
