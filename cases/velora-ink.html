Velora Ink & Beauty Academy

A responsive website concept for a beauty studio offering treatments and professional training.

The opening screen

I paired a close-up photograph with a script wordmark and warm, neutral colours. From the first screen, visitors can browse the services or enquire about an appointment.

From treatments to prices

Three cards introduce permanent makeup, lashes and fine line tattoos. Each links directly to the relevant section of the price list. A separate courses section gives students a route to the enquiry form.

On a phone, the treatment cards stack in a single column, with price links directly under each description. The courses section keeps its own image and enquiry button when the desktop split layout stacks.
