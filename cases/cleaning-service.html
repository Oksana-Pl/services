FRESH

A textile care website for carpet and mattress cleaning and hotel linen laundry, with a structured enquiry form.

Walkthrough of an earlier version of the website.

Find the right cleaning service

The services section separates carpet care, mattress care and hotel linen laundry. Each card combines a photograph, a short explanation and a direct quote link, so visitors can move from comparing services to making an enquiry.

Give the enquiry a clear structure

The quote form starts with service selection, followed by contact details, location and a short description. This gives visitors a place to explain what they need without having to compose a separate email.

Keep the first step visible on mobile

The mobile layout stacks the headline, service summary and quote button. Navigation moves into a menu, leaving the main action easy to find on a narrow screen.
